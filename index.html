II — CURRENTLY LEARNING

SAP FICO

IN PROGRESS

SAP FICO is the finance and controlling part of SAP ERP, where a company keeps its ledgers, payables, receivables, assets and cost data in one system. I am learning it to take my accounting practice further, alongside my BBA in Accounting and my work at Agro Village.

The learning path
STEP I

Foundations

Enterprise structure: company code, chart of accounts, fiscal year and the master data every posting depends on.

STEP II

General ledger

GL accounts, journal postings, document types and the period-end routine that closes the books.

STEP III

Payables and receivables

Vendor and customer accounts, invoices, payments and the clearing that keeps open items honest.

STEP IV

Assets and banking

Asset accounting from purchase to depreciation, and bank accounting and reconciliation.

STEP V

Controlling

Cost centres, profit centres, internal orders and profitability analysis (CO-PA).

STEP VI

Reporting and integration

Financial statements and management reports, and how FICO connects with purchasing and sales.
